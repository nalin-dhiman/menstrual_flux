# Research and governance notice

This package is an editable research-planning artifact, not a validated clinical system. It does not provide medical advice and must not be used for contraception, pregnancy planning, diagnosis, treatment, or triage.

Before collecting human-participant data, the project team must obtain applicable ethics approval, execute appropriate data-use agreements, define consent and withdrawal procedures, establish access and retention controls, and obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review. Menstrual, reproductive, wearable, medication, symptom, location-adjacent, and time-stamped data are sensitive and can remain re-identifiable after direct identifiers are removed.
